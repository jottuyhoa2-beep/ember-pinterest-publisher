Privacy Policy

Ember Pinterest Publisher · Effective date: November 10, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to Ember Pinterest Publisher (the "App"). The App is a personal publishing tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own Pinterest Business account, through the official Pinterest API (v5).

2. Data accessed

With the owner's authorization through Pinterest OAuth, the App accesses only the owner's own Pinterest account data needed to publish Pins: the owner's boards, Pins, and basic user account information (such as name, username, and profile information). The App never asks for, collects, or stores the owner's Pinterest password or session cookies.

3. How data is used

Account data is used only to: read the owner's boards, create and schedule Pins on the owner's boards, and check the owner's account and Pin status. Data is not used for any other purpose.

4. Data we do not collect

The App does not collect, store, or process personal data of any other Pinterest user. It does not collect payment information, location data, or contact lists.

5. Storage

Access tokens issued by Pinterest are stored securely by the platform that hosts the authorized connection, solely to keep the owner's authorized session working. No Pinterest account data is stored outside that platform, and no copies of Pins, boards, or account data are kept for any other purpose.

6. Sharing

The App does not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties. The only data sent outside the App is what the owner chooses to publish: Pins the owner creates are published to the owner's public Pinterest boards through the Pinterest API, and some Pins link to Amazon product pages through the owner's approved Amazon Associates account, with affiliate disclosure on the Pin.

7. Advertising

Account data accessed by the App is not used for advertising, profiling, or ad targeting.

8. Revocation

The owner may revoke the App's access at any time in Pinterest Settings, under the authorized apps / connected accounts section. Revoking access immediately stops the App from reading or publishing to the account.

9. Security

Access to Pinterest is made only through the official Pinterest OAuth flow over HTTPS. Access tokens are never shared publicly and never sent to any party other than Pinterest's API.

10. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date above will change when a new version is published.

Contact: the app owner, via the Pinterest Business account that owns this app.
