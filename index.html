About

Scent Stacking is a Pinterest collection about layering fragrance — vanilla, musk and amber — and about jelly beauty textures such as jelly blush. Every Pin is an original idea: a layering recipe to try, a texture close-up, or a gift idea for someone who loves scent.

How Pins are published

Pins are made by Ember, the owner's personal AI assistant, using Pinterest's official tools. Each Pin has an original AI-generated image (always marked as AI-modified), a title and a description — about 5 Pins a week. Some Pins link to this website, where you can find products the owner recommends on Amazon as an Amazon Associate. Every one of those Pins carries an affiliate disclosure.

Everything runs only on the owner's own account. Ember does not post on behalf of other users, and account data is never shared with third parties.
