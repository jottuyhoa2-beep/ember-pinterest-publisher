Ember Pinterest Publisher

A personal publishing tool for the owner's own Pinterest Business account.

Ember Pinterest Publisher connects the owner's Pinterest Business account to their personal AI assistant (Ember) to create and schedule original Pins for the owner's own boards about scent stacking (fragrance layering) and jelly beauty.

The app reads the account's boards, creates Pins with original AI-generated images, titles, and descriptions, and publishes them on a schedule of about 5 Pins per week. Some Pins link to Amazon product pages through the owner's approved Amazon Associates account, with affiliate disclosure.

The app only acts on the owner's own account. It does not post on behalf of other users, does not scrape Pinterest data, and does not share account data with third parties.
