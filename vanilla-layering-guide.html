How to layer vanilla perfume so it lasts all day

Vanilla is the easiest scent to layer, and the fastest to disappear. Spray it on dry skin in the morning and by lunch it is mostly gone. The fix is not more sprays. It is a base that holds the scent, and a second layer that stretches it.

1. Start with moisturised skin

Perfume sticks to oil and water, not to dry skin. Put on unscented body lotion first, then spray while your skin is still a little warm from the shower. Warm skin opens up and takes the scent in. Dry skin just lets it evaporate. This one step adds hours, and it costs nothing.

2. Spray less than you think

Two sprays are enough for vanilla: one at the base of the neck, one on a wrist. Hold the bottle about 15 cm away and let the mist settle. Do not rub your wrists together. Rubbing crushes the top notes and the scent turns flat. Let it dry on its own. It takes about a minute.

3. Add musk on top

Vanilla on its own fades by the afternoon. Musk does not. One light spray of a soft musk over the vanilla locks it in. Musk smells like warm skin, so it blends instead of competing. Spray the musk after the vanilla has dried, about five minutes later. The pair smells like you, only warmer.

4. Save amber for the evening

Amber is heavier than vanilla and musk. It works best after 5 pm, or on cooler days, when the warmth has room to breathe. One spray at the base of the neck is enough. On a hot day, skip the amber and let the vanilla and musk do the work.

5. The mistakes that kill vanilla

Three habits ruin a vanilla stack. Rubbing your wrists, which flattens the scent. Spraying on dry skin, which lets it vanish. And keeping the bottle in the bathroom, where heat and steam wear it down. Store it somewhere cool and dark, like a bedroom drawer, and it stays true for months.
